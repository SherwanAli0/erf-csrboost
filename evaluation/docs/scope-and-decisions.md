# Current scope and decisions

Author: Sherwan Ali. Version 0.3.0.

The local release includes the reusable package, CLI, static workbench, loopback
training interface, cloneable experimental estimators and a bounded research
pilot. Historical results and the original hosted application remain unchanged.

The 144-run pilot completed without failed or timed-out model cells. ERF's mean
AP gain over Balanced RF exceeded 0.01 on two of five families, while the median
fit-time ratio was 5.97. This failed the predeclared expansion rule. The product
direction therefore emphasizes evaluation, with ERF retained as experimental.

No larger superiority campaign, public release, deployment, billing change,
external user trial or claim of measured business benefit is included.
